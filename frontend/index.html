Quiz rules

Simple rules. A level playing field.

One locked answer per question
Answers accepted only before time expires
Correct answers earn 100 points plus speed
Quick incorrect answers can still earn speed points
Ties use correct answers, total time, then finish time
Quiz data is automatically removed after 24 hours
